Coastal Dynamics

Follow energy, momentum, and sediment from offshore forcing to a changing coast, and learn to see every intervention as a perturbation to a living system.

Written for students meeting this material for the first time. Coastal dynamics sits where fluid mechanics meets sediment transport, and that junction is where most readers get stuck. Every figure here can be pushed around until the mechanism is visible, and every number is checked against a published or analytic result, so what you are looking at can be trusted while you are still learning to judge it.

The organizing thesis

Coastal dynamics is the study of a feedback loop. Boundary forcing becomes nearshore flow; flow moves sediment; transport gradients reshape the coast; and the new geometry transforms the next wave and tide.

Read this book in two movements

Chapters 1–6 build the process chain: system, inheritance, forcing, hydrodynamics, and sediment. Chapters 7–10 let the morphology move: profile, coastline, inlets, and intervention.
