What this can and cannot claim

Strouhal & wake length are validated; drag is not. St and the wake recirculation length match the literature within a few percent. Absolute drag comes out high because a browser-sized domain confines the flow and inflates form drag — so Cd is reported as a diagnostic trend, not a pass/fail metric. A validation harness that only shows the numbers that pass is not validation.
2D laminar band only. The wake is genuinely 2D up to the Mode-A 3D instability at Re ≈ 188–190 — which is exactly why the Williamson correlation is defined over Re 49–180. Above it a 2D sim diverges from reality. Single-relaxation BGK also stiffens as τ → ½ (high Re); push Re and the field gets noisy.
