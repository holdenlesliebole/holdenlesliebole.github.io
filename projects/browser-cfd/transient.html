What this can and cannot claim

This is the 2D Orr mechanism, not the famous ~1000× growth. That figure is the 3D lift-up mechanism (streamwise vortices → streaks), which is driven by vortex tilting that cannot exist in 2D. A 2D sim honestly shows only the few-fold Orr gain. The full 3D story is the offline linear-operator / Manim companion (task #20).
The measured peak lands at the shear-predicted time t* — the mechanism is captured exactly. The peak gain sits below the unbounded inviscid Orr ceiling and the viscous Kelvin curve: a bounded, viscous, finite-resolution LBM dissipates the mode faster than the ideal (BGK numerical dissipation + wall confinement).
