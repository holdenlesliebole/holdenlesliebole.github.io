What this can and cannot claim

Weakly-compressible LBM, valid at low Mach (Ma ≈ ); error is O(Ma²). Walls use halfway bounce-back → 2nd-order for these flat, grid-aligned walls. 2D and laminar — turbulence is out of envelope (the 3D energy cascade needs vortex stretching, which vanishes in 2D). Steady Couette is laminar at every Re, so this case never approaches transition; it is the validation foundation and the substrate for the (later) transient-growth case.
